# 0032 Coupled-cluster equations come from an in-house Wick generator and run as one program over block tensors

Status: accepted 2026-10-30

## Context

M8 asks for CCSD, later (T), Λ and EOM, written once in spin-orbital form
over the block tensors of ADR-0030 and ADR-0031 so that every Fukutome
class, the point-group sectors and the k-points of a cell are served by
the same code. PLAN.md §5.5 left the source of the working equations
open: a Wick-contraction tool (pdaggerq, drudge with gristmill, SeQuant)
or a small in-house generator, with the generated code committed and
regenerated in CI. Four things had to be settled: where the equations
come from and in what form they are kept; how the terms are evaluated
over block tensors whose contraction is a two-operand `contract`; what
the Hamiltonian of the equations is and how its integrals are formed from
the fitted tensor; and how the amplitude iteration converges.

## Decision

- **An in-house generator derives the equations.** `hafnium.cc.generator`
  contracts normal-ordered operator strings over the Fermi vacuum by
  Wick's theorem: the fully contracted terms of ``<Φ_μ| H_N e^T |Φ>`` in
  which every cluster operator is linked to the Hamiltonian, with the
  general indices of ``H_N`` expanded into occupied and virtual ones and
  ``e^T`` into the products ``T1^m T2^n / (m! n!)`` the projection can
  absorb. Terms are brought to a canonical form, the dummies relabelled
  and the factors ordered over every sign-carrying permutation of the
  antisymmetric slots of ``<pq||rs>`` and ``t2``, and merged. The
  external tools were not taken: pdaggerq and SeQuant are C++ packages
  that would add a toolchain to the Rust-only build (ADR-0002), drudge
  needs a Spark-style runtime, and the derivation needed here is a few
  hundred lines whose output is checked against the textbook equations
  (the three energy terms, the fourteen singles terms and the thirty-one
  doubles orbits of the spin-orbital CCSD) and numerically.
- **The generated program is committed and regenerated by a test.**
  `python -m hafnium.cc.generator` writes `hafnium.cc._ccsd_equations`,
  a function per equation that evaluates the terms with pairwise
  `contract` calls; a unit test compares the committed file with a fresh
  generation, so drift between the generator and its output fails CI.
  The doubles residual is antisymmetric in ``i, j`` and ``a, b``, so its
  terms are grouped into orbits under those permutations: one term per
  orbit is evaluated and its images are permuted copies, which the
  generator verifies by checking that every image carries the
  coefficient the antisymmetry demands. Within a term the factors are
  contracted two at a time in the cheapest order under a fixed ratio of
  the virtual to the occupied extent; every index of a contracted term
  is free or shared by exactly two factors, so a pairwise contraction
  sums exactly the shared indices. Intermediates common to several terms
  are recomputed, a later optimization.
- **The Hamiltonian is the normal-ordered one of the reference, with the
  integrals formed in full.** The equations read the blocks
  ``f[oo]``, ``f[ov]``, ``f[vo]``, ``f[vv]`` of the Fock operator, which
  for a converged Hartree–Fock reference is diagonal in its canonical
  orbitals with the orbital energies, and the nine blocks ``<pq||rs>``
  of the antisymmetrized integrals they need, ``<vv||vv>`` included.
  `FittedOrbitalTensors` keeps the fitted tensor between every pair of
  orbital spaces, plain and signed for a cell, and assembles
  ``<pq||rs> = (pr|qs) − (ps|qr)`` of any block from them, which MP2 now
  shares. The Fock operator of a cell is that of the mean field with its
  Ewald exchange correction (ADR-0017), as for MP2, so that the first
  CCSD iteration is the MP2 energy and the two methods describe the
  same Hamiltonian; a DF-driven ladder that never forms ``<vv||vv>``
  and a non-Hartree–Fock reference with an off-diagonal Fock operator
  are later extensions.
- **The amplitudes iterate with DIIS on the update.** Each iteration
  adds ``R / Δ`` to the amplitudes, ``Δ`` the orbital energy denominators,
  and extrapolates the singles and doubles together with Pulay's method
  over the real inner products of the updates, through the solver of the
  SCF's DIIS. The iteration starts from zero singles and the first-order
  doubles or from given amplitudes, converges on the update norm and the
  energy change, and raises `ConvergenceError` with its last state as the
  SCF does.

## Consequences

- One CCSD for the eight classes, the point-group sectors and k-points:
  the energies agree across the classes to 1e-11 Hartree, equal full
  configuration interaction for two electrons, match PySCF's DF-CCSD
  (RHF, UHF, and GHF on the same fitted integrals) to 3e-11 Hartree, and
  over a 3×1×1 mesh match PySCF's KRCCSD with ``keep_exxdiv`` and the
  supercell at Γ to 1e-11 per cell.
- PySCF's k-point CCSD classes drop the Ewald exchange correction from
  the Fock matrix by default and ``KUCCSD`` and ``KGCCSD`` cannot keep it,
  which shifts their correlation energy by 5 mHartree per cell for the
  helium test cell against their own ``KMP2``; the comparison sets
  ``keep_exxdiv`` on ``KRCCSD`` and switches the correction off on both
  sides for the other two classes (a Madelung constant of zero in
  Hafnium's exchange term, ``exxdiv=None`` in PySCF), where all three
  agree with PySCF to 1e-10 per cell.
- The full ``<vv||vv>`` block costs ``n_v⁴`` words, 20 GB for benzene in
  def2-SVP as a generalized state, so the generic path serves small and
  medium systems until the DF-driven ladder lands; the recomputed
  intermediates cost a constant factor on the ``o³v³`` terms. On 16
  cores the CCSD step takes 0.2–0.5 s for water in def2-SVP in every
  class (PySCF 0.4–1.2 s), 63 s for benzene in def2-SVP as a restricted
  state against PySCF's spin-adapted, hand-optimized RCCSD at 18 s, the
  factor the plan foresaw for the spin-orbital route, and 34 s for the
  helium cell over a 3×3×1 mesh against KRCCSD at 10 s, where the Python
  loop over the 276 000 block products of an iteration dominates and
  batching the blocks of a k-point structure is the lever.
- Later: (T) from the same generator with batching over the occupied
  triples, the Λ equations and EOM-CC, common-subexpression elimination
  and a path optimizer across terms, the spin-adapted RCCSD fast path if
  measured to matter, and the point operations of a space group as
  further generators.
