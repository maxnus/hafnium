# 0033 The (T) correction comes from the same generator and runs over windows of the occupied axis

Status: accepted 2026-10-31

## Context

M8 asks for (T) on top of the CCSD of ADR-0032, for every Fukutome
class, with point-group sectors and over k-points, from the same
generated equations. Four things had to be settled: how the triples
expressions are derived, since the disconnected term of (T) is not a
connected coupled-cluster residual; how the triples are evaluated when
``t3`` never fits (``o³v³`` words, 4 TB for benzene in def2-SVP as a
spin-orbital tensor) over block tensors whose axes run over whole
sectors; which energy expression is taken for complex orbitals; and how
the orbital energy denominators know the space of an index once an
index runs over part of a space.

## Decision

- **The generator derives the triples, connected and disconnected.**
  The triples projection ``<Φ_ijk^abc| = <Φ| a†_i a†_j a†_k a_c a_b a_a``
  joins the projections of ADR-0032, and the expectation value keeps
  either the terms in which every cluster operator is contracted with
  the Hamiltonian or the others. The connected triples
  ``W = <Φ_ijk^abc| H_N T2 |Φ>_C`` are the eighteen terms of the
  textbook ``P(i/jk) P(a/bc) [Σ_e t_jk^ae <ei||bc> − Σ_m t_im^bc <ma||jk>]``,
  the disconnected ``V = <Φ_ijk^abc| H_N (T1 + T2) |Φ>`` the eighteen of
  ``P(i/jk) P(a/bc) [t_i^a <jk||bc> + f_ai t_jk^bc]``, the Fock term of
  which vanishes for a canonical reference and costs nothing to keep.
  The correction is ``E(T) = 1/36 Σ conj(W + V) W / D``, the
  fourth-order ``<Φ| T2† H_N T3 |Φ>`` and fifth-order
  ``<Φ| T1† H_N T3 |Φ>`` energies with ``T3 = W / D``: the bra supplies
  the conjugate, which is PySCF's convention too; its real part is
  taken, as for the CCSD energy. The terms are written to a second
  generated module, ``hafnium.cc._ccsd_pt_equations``, regenerated by
  the same drift test.
- **Tensor axes have windows, and the triples are evaluated over
  them.** A `TensorAxis` carries the start of every sector within the
  axis it is a window of; a window restricts one set of related
  sectors, a block with its derived partner at the same point and their
  images under the symmetries of the state, to the same slice and
  leaves every other sector empty, so that a tensor over a window keeps
  the symmetries and the derived blocks of the full structure, and
  `BlockTensor.window` slices the blocks as views. The generated triples
  functions take one window per occupied free index and restrict every
  operand to the windows of the letters it carries; the image of a term
  under a permutation ``g`` of the occupied indices is the representative
  evaluated over the windows of ``g(l)`` and permuted by ``g⁻¹``, so each
  coset of an orbit is one contraction instead of a permuted copy. The
  occupied axis is cut into windows of a number of orbitals per sector,
  by default the largest number whose batch of triples is estimated to
  fit a memory limit, and the correction is summed over the triples
  ``I ≤ J ≤ K`` of windows, each counted as often as its orderings,
  since the summand is symmetric under the permutations of ``i, j, k``;
  over k-points the result is divided by the number of points, as the
  CCSD energy is.
- **The denominators take the spaces of the axes from the caller.** An
  axis that is a window of the occupied orbitals, or that holds as many
  orbitals as the other space, is a window of both spaces by its sizes,
  so `OrbitalSpaces.denominators` now takes the space of every axis and
  checks the axis against it, and MP2 and CCSD name their spaces. This
  removes a latent misidentification: with as many virtual as occupied
  orbitals (H₂ in STO-3G) the virtual axis was read as occupied, which
  gave wrong MP2 denominators, while CCSD still converged to its fixed
  point.

## Consequences

- One (T) for the eight classes, the point-group sectors and k-points:
  the corrections agree across the classes to 1e-11 Hartree, vanish for
  two electrons, are independent of the window, match a dense
  spin-orbital evaluation of the textbook formula, and match PySCF's
  CCSD(T) on the same fitted integrals for RHF, UHF and GHF references
  and its k-point ``KRCCSD`` with ``keep_exxdiv`` and ``KGCCSD`` without
  the exchange correction (ADR-0032) to 1e-10 per cell; PySCF has no
  (T) for ``KUCCSD``, so that class is checked against the other two and
  the supercell at Γ.
- PySCF's ``KGCCSD`` shifts the occupied orbital energies of its
  denominators by the Madelung constant whatever the mean field's
  ``exxdiv``, a device for the convergence of the CCSD iteration that
  leaves its fixed point alone but enters (T) directly, where it moves
  the helium cell's correction by 8 μHartree per cell; the comparison
  evaluates PySCF's (T) with the diagonal of the Fock matrix of the
  equations, which is what ``KRCCSD`` keeps with ``keep_exxdiv``.
- On 16 cores the (T) step of water in def2-SVP takes 0.3–2 s in the
  three real classes against PySCF's 0.03 s (restricted, spin-adapted
  and compiled), 0.2 s (unrestricted) and 0.9 s (generalized); the
  generalized class pays the Python loop over the blocks of the window
  tensors and the permuted copies, batched over the windows the memory
  limit allows. For the helium cell over 3×1×1 the step takes 1.4 s
  (restricted), 1.2 s (unrestricted) and 5.6 s (generalized) against
  PySCF's KRCCSD(T) at 1.2 s and KGCCSD(T) at 8.0 s.
- A batch costs eighteen contractions of a block tensor over three
  windows with a block of the integrals or the amplitudes, the
  ``o³v⁴`` count of the standard algorithm, plus eighteen outer products
  for ``V``; with windows of one orbital the Python loop over the blocks
  dominates for small systems, so the memory limit, not the smallest
  window, is the default.
- Later: windows over the virtual axes for the ``<vv||vv>``-free
  ladder, the stabilizer of a term's occupied pair to halve the cosets,
  the Λ equations, and the spin-adapted (T) if measured to matter.
